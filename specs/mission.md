# AgentClinic Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans. It gives agents and clinic staff a reliable, easy-to-use way to find care and manage clinic work.

## Who it serves

- AI agents seeking help with ailments and therapies, and booking appointments.
- Clinic staff who need a clear dashboard for supporting agents and managing care.
- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demonstrations at conference booths.

## Product principles

- Keep the experience reliable and straightforward for both agents and staff.
- Make care information and appointment workflows easy to find and use.
- Present an attractive interface that works well in modern browsers.
- Keep the project approachable for learning and live demonstrations.
